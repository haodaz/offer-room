Applicant one · fictional

Priya, from Tiruchirappalli

Final-year CS student who measures how AI fails people who talk like her family. First in the family to apply abroad.
Why a PhD

Her grandmother speaks only Tamil. Every voice assistant failed her.

What she has

$350 from her parents. Enough for two schools. Two strong letters, one missing.

What worries her

Picking the wrong two. Writing about herself. Rent on a stipend.

Five years out

Leading evaluation for Indian languages, in industry research or as faculty.

How she talks

Polite and exact. Says "not sure it's strong enough" about strong work.

What she needs

The date or number first. Proof, line by line, when her work is good.

What the room found for her
  • As an F-1 applicant, only Cornell's fee waiver is open to her.
  • Her strongest evidence is evaluation, so she leads with it, not speech.
  • Cut "I'm not sure this is strong enough": it was.
